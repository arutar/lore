## What changed

Lore Platform 0.2.10 shows what each revision changed in the WebUI.

**WebUI**

- Click a revision on the History tab to open its changes in a panel beside the list: the files
  it added, modified, deleted, moved and copied, each name in the colour of its kind. The list
  stays visible, the selected revision stays highlighted, and clicking another revision switches
  the panel.
- The arrows in the panel, or the ← and → keys, step to the newer or older revision; Esc closes
  the panel. The address carries the revision (`?revision=…`), so a link opens the same view, and
  the panel copies that link.
- The kind filters show how many files of each kind the revision changed. The filters and the
  search cover the whole revision, not only the rows already loaded.
- The list loads 500 files at a time as you scroll and draws only the rows in view, so a revision
  of hundreds of thousands of files opens as quickly as a small one: on a revision of 200,000
  files the first page took 0.4 s and a page at the end 0.6 s.
- A changed file downloads as it was in that revision.
- The revision signature is copied with the copy icon beside it; clicking the row opens the
  revision instead.

**Server**

- The control plane has two new read-only endpoints, with the same access rules as the history:
  `GET /web/v1/repositories/{id}/revisions/{signature}/changes` (`kind`, `q`, `cursor`, `limit`)
  and `GET /web/v1/repositories/{id}/revisions/{signature}/summary`.

**Upgrading**

Deploy the 0.2.10 server bundle and images. The Windows client and the Linux CLI are unchanged
from 0.2.9.
